Fabrication & Quality Assurance

In-House Workshop & Engineering Standards

Unlike trading intermediaries, VWELDCORE fabricates, assembles, and tests core thermal and stainless systems in-house at our Abule Egba facility.

01

AISI 304 / 316L Sanitary Welding

Precision TIG and MIG welding with ground, crevice-free food-contact seams for dairy, meat processing, pharma, and beverage operations.

02

Tropicalized Thermal Design

Every cold room, blast freezer, and brine ice plant is sized for +35°C to +43°C Nigerian ambient temperatures and generator/grid power cycles.

03

Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)

All chillers, ice block machines, and piping undergo nitrogen pressure testing, vacuum dehydration, and temperature pull-down logging prior to dispatch.
