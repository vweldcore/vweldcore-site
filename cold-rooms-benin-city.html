Benin City FAQs

Do you install in Benin City?

Yes. We fabricate at our Abule Egba, Lagos workshop, then deliver, install and commission on site in Benin City, Edo State.

How fast can I get a quote for Benin City?

Quote requests get an engineering response within 24 hours. Send dimensions, target temperature, capacity and power details for the fastest BOQ.

What power supply do I need?

Small walk-in chillers can run on single-phase 220-240V; blast freezers, 800L chillers and ice plants need 3-phase 380-415V from utility or generator.
