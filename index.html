About VWELDCORE Innovations

VWELDCORE Innovations Nigeria Limited (RC: 8909233) is a Lagos-based industrial engineering firm specializing in cold storage systems, hygienic stainless steel fabrication, and commercial refrigeration. Our work includes government and World Bank-assisted institutional projects, delivered to NAFDAC/GMP compliance standards. We serve clients across Nigeria and are open to international inquiries and export partnerships.

ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES

01 / REFRIGERATION

Industrial Cold Storage Solutions

Custom designing and installing robust cold rooms, blast freezers, and pharmaceutical walk-in chillers. Engineered utilizing high-density cam-locking polyurethane (PU) insulation profiles to maintain rigorous thermal baselines under volatile ambient temperatures.

  • ✔ Digital temperature monitoring & automation alerts
  • ✔ Reinforced structural floor configurations for forklift access
  • ✔ Heavy-duty airtight sliding processing doors
02 / FABRICATION

Stainless Steel & Structural Welding

Heavy manufacturing structural setups using pristine corrosion-resistant sheets and tubing. Precision argon TIG welding systems configured specifically for high-hygiene food processing conveyors, tables, mixing vats, and factory floor sorting structures.

  • ✔ Smooth anti-bacterial welded seam finishes
  • ✔ Tailored commercial kitchen setups & drainage solutions
  • ✔ Rugged industrial structural steel framing
