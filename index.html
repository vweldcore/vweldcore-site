Procurement & Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common engineering, material, and delivery questions.

How quickly do you provide a formal quotation or BOQ?

We respond to all quote requests within 24 hours. For standard ice machines, chillers, and modular cold rooms, we provide immediate budgetary pricing; for custom industrial plants, we schedule a rapid site/drawing review.

What is the difference between Grade 304 and Grade 316 stainless steel?

Grade 304 is the industry standard for food processing, commercial kitchens, dairy, and cold room shelving. Grade 316 (with added molybdenum) is recommended for high-salinity, acidic, chemical, or pharmaceutical environments requiring extra corrosion resistance.

What insulation thickness do you use for cold rooms and blast freezers?

We use high-density polyurethane (PU) panels (40–42 kg/m³): typically 75mm–100mm for positive chillers (+2°C to +15°C), 100mm–120mm for frozen storage (-18°C to -25°C), and 150mm for rapid blast freezers (-35°C to -40°C).

Do you install and maintain projects outside Lagos?

Yes. While our fabrication workshop is at Olota Junction, Abule Egba, Lagos, we fabricate, transport, and install cold rooms, ice plants, and stainless tanks across all 36 states in Nigeria and West Africa.

What power supply is required for your cold rooms and ice machines?

Small walk-in chillers can operate on single-phase (220V–240V) or 3-phase power, while industrial blast freezers, 800L chillers, and 100–200 block ice machines run on 3-phase (380V–415V, 50Hz) utility or diesel generator supply.
