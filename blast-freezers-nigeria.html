Frequently Asked Questions

Blast freezer or storage freezer?

A blast freezer drives product temperature down quickly with high airflow (-35°C to -40°C). A storage freezer holds frozen product at -18°C to -25°C. Many plants use both.

Can a blast freezer run on a generator?

Industrial units run on 3-phase supply from utility or a correctly sized diesel generator. Generator sizing is covered in your BOQ.
